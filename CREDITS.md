# Credits

## Anatomy

The 3D body and head (`body-*.js`) are built from **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The meshes were reduced and packed for the web. The craniotomy lesion, the surgical plan and the cortical veins in the 3D rendering module were drawn for this demo case; they are not patient imaging.
